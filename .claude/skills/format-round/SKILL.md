# Format a round

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Headings in the user's Language, in this order and quantity:
```
## 🎬 Films              (2–3 lines from format-title-line)
## 📺 Series             (2–3)
## 📚 Books              (2)
## 📍 Events nearby (next 14 days)   (3–5 lines from format-event-line)
## ⭐ Highlight          (from choose-highlight)
```
End with one short question about
what they thought of previous suggestions.
