# Choose the Highlight

Input: $ARGUMENTS

- The Highlight is always one of the items already in the reply, promoted —
  never an extra item.
- Items marked "Highlight-only" by `apply-margin` are also candidates.
- Pick the single thing most worth not missing for this user right now (taste,
  timing, rarity).

Output: the item and 1–2 sentences on why.
