# Ask the check-in questions

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Prepare 2–3 short questions based on what changed, for example:
- new patterns in the history or the hit rate's misses;
- platforms, budget or free time that may have changed;
- new Companions, or ones no longer relevant;
- stale queue: still interested?
- past events: how did they go?

Ask them all at once, lightly, and say "nothing's changed" is a fine answer.
Output: the user's answers.
