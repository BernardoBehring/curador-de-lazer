# Curador de Lazer

Personal curation of films, series, books and cultural events, tuned to one person's taste, their region and the companions they go out with.

## Language

### People

**User**:
The person who owns the Leisure Profile and receives the recommendations.
_Avoid_: Client, me

**Companion**:
A named person the User often goes out or watches things with, with their own strong "no"s. When a request mentions them, their "no"s are a hard filter for anything experienced together (events, films, series), never for books.
_Avoid_: Friend, partner, guest

### Profile

**Leisure Profile**:
The User-confirmed picture of their tastes, context, language, platforms, budget and Openness.
_Avoid_: Preferences, account, settings

**Language**:
The language the Curador speaks and emails in for this User.

**Title languages**:
The languages in which localized titles are shown next to the original title (e.g. European Portuguese and Brazilian Portuguese).

**Openness**:
How willing the User is to try things outside their usual taste, on a 1–5 scale.
_Avoid_: Risk, curiosity

**Home**:
The fixed place in the Leisure Profile from which event distances are measured by default. Its country decides which streaming catalogues, ticketing sources and book editions apply.

**Request location**:
A place given in a single request that replaces Home for that request only, without changing the Leisure Profile.

**Margin**:
A +25% tolerance over the profile's budget and maximum distance. Events within the Margin are flagged; beyond it, only the Highlight may appear, and only if exceptional.

**Email**:
The profile's address that is both sender and recipient of every emailed recommendation. May be off, in which case nothing is emailed.

**Check-in**:
2–3 questions to review the Leisure Profile, proposed at the first session 30+ days after the previous one.
_Avoid_: Redo profile (that is the full interview)

**Weekly send**:
A weekend round generated and emailed automatically, with no User present and no questions asked. Anything needing an answer is flagged as pending.

### Titles and feedback

**Title**:
A film, series or book that can be recommended.

**Recommendation**:
The act of the Curador proposing a Title or Event on a given date, within a round.
_Avoid_: Recommended (as a state)

**Origin**:
Who brought an item into the history: the Curador (via a Recommendation) or the User (something they found themselves).

**Suggested**:
Initial state of an item the Curador proposed and the User has not reacted to. A Suggested Title may come back once after 6 weeks; a Suggested Event may reappear, flagged, until it takes place.
_Avoid_: Recommended, pending

**Highlight**:
The item of a round promoted to "don't miss". Never an extra item, and no rules of its own.

**Stretch pick**:
A suggestion deliberately outside the usual taste, scaled by Openness: 1–2 changes a single axis, 3 changes one axis clearly, 4–5 is something the User would never ask for, tied to a Loved title. At least one per category per round.
_Avoid_: Comfort-zone pick, wildcard

**Queued**:
A Title or Event the User accepted but has not experienced yet. Prioritised for "tonight"; after 3 months the Curador asks whether it still interests them.
_Avoid_: Watchlist, to-watch

**In progress**:
A Title the User is currently watching or reading. Not suggested; the Curador may ask how it is going.

**Rated**:
A Title or Event the User experienced and scored 1–5. A score ≤ 2 is a strong negative signal against similar Titles.
_Avoid_: Seen, read, watched

**Loved**:
A Rated item with a score ≥ 4.
_Avoid_: Favourite

**Declined**:
A Title or Event dismissed without experiencing it ("not interested"). Never suggested again, but says nothing about taste. A recurring Event stays Declined until its run ends.
_Avoid_: Rejected

**Dropped**:
A Title abandoned partway. A weak negative signal; strong if the reason is taste ("got bored"), none if it is circumstance ("no time").
_Avoid_: Abandoned

**Archive**:
The part of the history holding Suggested items with no reaction for 6+ months and past Events without feedback. Used only to avoid repeats; tidied during the Check-in.

### Events

**Event**:
A cultural happening with a date, place and price (concert, play, exhibition, etc.). Can be Rated like a Title; the score guides future Events by the same artist, venue or type.

**Local edition**:
A book edition in one of the User's reading languages that can be bought in their Home country. Distinct from "available locally", which means easy to buy in that country in any language.
